Why this design

  • Uniform product makes downstream test results reproducible
  • Gap-adjustable: several product sizes from one machine

Frequently asked questions

Roll crusher vs jaw crusher in a prep chain?

Jaw first for lump reduction, roll second for controlled product. Together they give a reproducible feed for any test programme.

Can it be skirted for dust?

Yes — ask for the sealed variant with your quotation.
