Frequently asked questions

Why dry separation for lab work?

Many ores are first verified dry — especially where the plant will run an HPGR dry line or water is scarce. Dry lab data also avoids the moisture variable when comparing samples.

Can the field strength be changed later?

The standard unit ships at 3,500 Gauss; other field strengths are set at order. Tell us your ore and we will recommend the setting.
