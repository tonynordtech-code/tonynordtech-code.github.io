RXGD Multi-Channel Automatic Slurry Sampler

Three streams in one unit — raw ore, concentrate and tailings sampled around the clock

Get a quotation → WhatsApp us

The RXGD is a three-channel automatic slurry sampler with two to three sampler sets: it takes raw ore, concentrate and tailings on a timed cycle from a single installation. 316L stainless sampling tubes with ceramic lining and a UHMWPE distribution box stand up to corrosive copper and gold duties. Pneumatically driven; controlled by timer relay as standard, with PLC + touch screen optional.

Metallurgical accounting on concentrator circuitsNight-shift and weekend sampling coverageCopper and gold concentrators (corrosive duties)Pilot plants and laboratory circuits
